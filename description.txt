Overview
--------
You are Joe working at the Sideways factory, you must use your bucket to collect the slop that is slowly oozing out of the pipes.
Pressing fire make joe bang the pipe to make the slop ooze out faster.
When the slop is in the bucket, joe must deposit the slop into the correct coloured tub, red slop goes into the red tub.
The tubs are on a conveyour belt, moving right to left. The conveyer belt is at the bottom of the screen.

The slop will drop onto the floor and disappear if not collected,  missed slop will increase Joe's 'cynicism meter'
When Joes cynicism meter hits Max (0-100), he will hand his notice in and quit.
Occassionally a vending machine treat will appear, eg coffee, chocolate bar, fruit, collecting this will decrease Joe's cyncism and score points.


Scoring: collecting slop, banging the pipe, depositing slop into the correct tub, collecting treats, stomping cockroaches.

Nota Bene
---------
Pipes can dispense different colours


The gane is split into 5 stages,

Level 1 'Apprenticeship'
Easy, slow slop, 2 colours red and blue, 2 pipes, 2 types of tub. Must collect 5 lots of slop.

Level 2 'Get on with it!'
4 pipes, 3 colours, 6 types of slop need to be collected, , slop oozes slightly faster

Level 3 'El Stompo'
4 pipes, 4 colours, 10 types of slop, slop oozes slightly faster
cockroaches appear - they slowly increase you cynicism, Joe must walk up to them and press fire to stomp them

Level 4 'Meet Mr Knut'
4 pipes, 5 colours/tubs, 10 to collect slop, slop oozes slightly faster
Mr Knut (Joes manager) makes an appearance, he will move around the screen in a random pattern, if Joe bumps into him Mr Knut will tell joe off for a few seconds (delaying Joe) and increase his cynicism.

Level 5 'Knackered'
6 pipes, 7 colours, 7 tub types, conveyer belt speeds up, slop ooze speeds up
New Character appears, Chairmain Fumduck, he acts like Mr Knut but applies more damage (i mean cynicism) to joe.


Completing this level finishes the game and Joe retires with a cheap gold clock and then shortly dies due to ill heath.
If Joe's cynicism hits maximum, he storms into the Chairman's office and tells him to 'To shove the job where the sun don't shine', Joe retires to a nice sunny beach.
